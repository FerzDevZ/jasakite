# Deep Research Methodology & Query Decomposition

## 4-Step Investigation Framework

1. **Breadth-First Exploration**: Launch 5 diverse search queries spanning industry docs, benchmarks, and community discussions.
2. **Depth-First Probing**: For key findings, trace references to original GitHub commits, RFCs, or benchmark data.
3. **Contradiction Analysis**: Compare metrics across different environments to eliminate bias.
4. **Synthesis Matrix**: Summarize trade-offs in structured Markdown comparison tables.
